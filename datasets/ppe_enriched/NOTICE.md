## Labels

Bounding-box labels in this release are derived from each source's own annotations by a documented, version-controlled class mapping (`tools/build_atharion_release.py`). They are derivative works of the source annotations and inherit the licence of the image they describe. No annotation was invented.

Because this release mixes CC BY 4.0 and AGPL-3.0 material, redistribute the whole under terms compatible with both, keep this NOTICE with it, and attribute every source listed above.
